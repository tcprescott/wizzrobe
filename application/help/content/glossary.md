## A–C

**Accommodation**: something staff arrange so you can take part, like step-free
access or extra time between matches. You ask for one from your Profile. See
[Asking for an accommodation](/help/accessibility).

**Acknowledge**: telling staff you've seen something and you're good with it.
Players acknowledge a match they've been scheduled into, and crew acknowledge an
approved slot. The crew button says *Confirm*, but it does the same thing.
Acknowledging isn't checking in.

**Admin**: the Admin Desk, the staff member overseeing the tournament room.
Checking matches in, flagged results, no-shows, disputes and who may come into
the room are the admin's call. Everyone else who runs the event is called staff.

**Archived event**: a past event, kept read-only so its brackets and results
stay up. Nobody can change anything in it, staff included, until staff unarchive
it. See [Archiving last year's event](/help/staff-events#archiving-last-year-s-event).

**Ask to change**: the item in a match's ⋮ menu on My Schedule that asks staff to
move or call off one of your matches. Nothing changes until staff answer. Your
requests and their answers are listed under *Your change requests* on My
Schedule.

**Asset**: one piece of loaner gear in the equipment register, with its own
number and QR code. Only if your community lends equipment. See [Borrowing
equipment](/help/equipment).

**Async qualifier**: a qualifier you play on your own time within a window,
instead of against an opponent at a set time. Only if your community runs them.
See [Async qualifiers](/help/qualifiers).

**Attendee**: someone at the event in person. Meetups and accommodation
requests use this word. *Member* means you belong to the community in the app.

**Bracket**: what decides who plays whom. In a bracket-run tournament, matchups
come from the bracket instead of from match requests.

**Broadcast tech**: the people who run a stage, covering setup, capture and the
stream. A stage belongs to them, not a proctor, and it has its own rules,
separate from the tournament room's.

**Broadcaster**: crew who run the stream for one match, on an event that has
them turned on. Anyone in the community can sign up, the same way as
commentators. Not the same as broadcast tech, a staff role for a whole stage.

**Check out / check in (equipment)**: borrowing a piece of loaner gear and
recording that it's back. Anyone signed in can check gear out to themselves, but
only equipment managers and staff can check it back in. This has nothing to do
with checking in for a match or picking up your badge at the check-in desk. See
[Borrowing something](/help/equipment#borrowing-something).

**Check-in (a match)**: when a match is recorded as ready to start, with both
players seated. It's per match, not per player, and a proctor or an admin does
it, not you. Don't mix it up with *event check-in* or checking equipment in.

**Commentator**: crew who talk over a match on stream.

**Comp / comped**: getting into the event without buying a badge, because of a
role (usually staff) or enough scheduled volunteer hours. See [Comped staff and
volunteers](/help/check-in#comped-staff-and-volunteers).

**Confirm (a result)**: staff making a recorded result official. It moves a match
from {state:Finished} to {state:Confirmed}. This is different from a crew member
confirming they can cover a slot.

**Crew**: commentators and trackers, plus broadcasters on an event that has
them. Crew is per match, and separate from
volunteering, which is per shift.

**Crew coordinator**: the person for a tournament who approves its crew signups,
along with staff and the tournament's admin.

## D–P

**Default event**: the event a link with no event in it opens. A community has
exactly one, and staff pick it on Admin → Events.

**Display name**: the name people see next to your matches, on stream and in
crew lists. Set it on your Profile. If you leave it blank, your Discord username
is used.

**Draft event**: an event staff are still setting up. Only staff, and people
given a role in it, can see it until it goes live.

**Enrolled / opted in**: see *Signed up*.

**Equipment manager**: the person who runs the equipment desk, lending gear out
and checking it back in. Staff can do the same. See [Running the equipment
desk](/help/equipment#running-the-equipment-desk).

**Event**: one run of what a community holds, like SGL 2026: its tournaments,
matches, stages, shifts and check-in. The header's event button switches
between them when there's more than one, and the Event tab shows the one you
picked. See [More than one event](/help/getting-started#more-than-one-event).

**Event check-in**: arriving at the event and picking up your lanyard at the
check-in desk. Separate from a match's check-in. See [Checking in when you
arrive](/help/check-in#checking-in-when-you-arrive).

**Harder settings**: a tougher preset some tournaments offer next to their
standard one. A match only uses it when every player privately opts in.

**Join request**: how you ask to become a member of a community that doesn't let
people straight in. Staff approve or decline it, and you get a message either
way.

**Lanyard**: what the check-in desk gives you, one per person. It's Staff,
Volunteer, VIP, Base or Day Pass, whichever is the highest you qualify for. See
[Checking in when you arrive](/help/check-in#checking-in-when-you-arrive).

**Late**: the status on a match that's past its scheduled time with nobody
checked in. Proctor Station puts these first, under *Late, check in now*. See
[The board](/help/proctor#the-board).

**Meetup**: a get-together, panel or side event an attendee runs and lists on
Home › Event › Meetups. It's not official programming. See
[Meetups](/help/community-events).

**Member**: someone who belongs to a community. You need to be one to see its
schedule, sign up for its tournaments or be scheduled. Signing in isn't enough
on its own.

**Needs crew**: the switch above Event › Schedule that shows only matches still
on a stage and still short of approved crew. A match that's still short also says
*needs crew* next to its crew count. See [Signing up as crew](/help/crew#signing-up).

**Needs review**: a finished match a proctor flagged for an admin to look at. Its
status on Admin › Schedule and Proctor Station says *Flagged*, and the match's own
dialog shows a *Needs review* chip. Confirming the result clears it. See [Running
a match](/help/proctor#running-a-match).

**Offer for stream**: the item in a match's ⋮ menu on My Schedule that tells staff
you'd be happy for the match to be streamed. It doesn't book anything.

**Par**: on an async qualifier seed, the average of the fastest approved times.
Finishing at par scores 100, and par moves as more runs are approved. See
[Scoring and the leaderboard](/help/qualifiers#scoring-and-the-leaderboard).

**Pool**: a group of async qualifier seeds, with a set number of runs per player.
See [Pools and runs](/help/qualifiers#pools-and-runs).

**Proctor**: a volunteer who runs matches in a tournament room. They seat players
at stations, start matches and record results. They don't confirm results,
reschedule matches, approve crew or run matches on a stage. See [Running matches
as a proctor](/help/proctor).

**Profile reminder**: the red banner at the top of every page when your community
wants something on your Profile that's missing, like pronouns or a linked
Challonge account. Its button takes you to the field.

## R–Z

**Race room**: the racetime.gg room an online match is played in. When it opens
you get a DM, and a **Join the room** button shows at the top of My Schedule.

**Reattempt**: voiding one of your async qualifier runs to free its slot for a
fresh draw. You get a seed you haven't played in that pool, unless there are
none left. See [Reattempts](/help/qualifiers#reattempts).

**Request Match**: the button on My Schedule for booking a match yourself, in a
tournament that allows it.

**Retired (equipment)**: an item taken out of use. It stays in the register but
can't be borrowed. See [Running the equipment
desk](/help/equipment#running-the-equipment-desk).

**Room seeds screen**: a shared screen in a tournament room showing the seed for
every match about to be played. It opens from a private link staff give out for
each machine, it's read-only, and a match drops off once it starts. See [The
room's seeds screen](/help/proctor#the-room-s-seeds-screen).

**RSVP**: on a meetup that takes RSVPs, telling the host you're coming.
You also get the reminder DM. See [Getting a reminder or
RSVPing](/help/community-events#getting-a-reminder-or-rsvping).

**Run sheet**: how the schedule boards (Event › Schedule, My Schedule, Admin ›
Schedule and Proctor Station) lay out matches. Each match gets one line, grouped
by where it stands (*Live now*, *Up next* and so on), with only the buttons you
use most. Tap a line to open the match's details, like the seed, stations and
full crew list.

**Seed**: the randomised game the match is played on. When it's rolled you get a
*Seed ready* DM, and it shows on the *Seed* line when you tap the match on My
Schedule, the schedule or Proctor Station. It's a link, or for randomizers you
paste into (Wind Waker, Zelda 1) the seed itself with a *Copy* button.

**Signed up**: you're in a tournament's player pool, either from the Tournaments
tab or through a linked Challonge bracket. You have to be signed up before you
can be scheduled in it. Also called *enrolled* or *opted in*.

**Stage**: where a match is played and streamed from, run by the broadcast techs
instead of a proctor. The board's *Stage* column and the admin *Stages* list are
the same set of places.

**Station**: the numbered seat you play at. You're given one at check-in, and it
then shows in the match's details on the schedule board.

**Stream candidate**: a match staff might stream but haven't put on a stage yet.
Its *Stage* says *Candidate* on the schedule boards, and the *Streamed, plus
possible streams* match alert DMs you about these.

**Suggest a time**: the button in the scheduling dialogs that picks a slot
neither player has blocked out and the room has space for. It fills in the form
but doesn't book anything. See [How Suggest a time works](/help/suggest-a-time).

**Tournament admin**: the person who runs a particular tournament. They can do
most of what staff can, but only in their own tournament. Not the same as the
Admin Desk in the tournament room.

**Tournament room**: a room of stations where matches are played off stream, run
by a proctor. It's the counterpart to a stage. The two have different rules, and
the event sets those, so look under Event Information if your community
publishes it.

**Tracker**: crew who run the item and progress tracking viewers see next to the
game.

**Volunteer**: someone who works scheduled shifts. Having the Volunteer role is
what puts you in the pool coordinators schedule from. See
[Volunteering](/help/volunteering).

**Walk-up**: someone checked in at the desk without a Matcherino badge. Only
staff can add them. See [Walk-ups](/help/check-in#walk-ups).

**Watch**: following a match you're not in, so you get a DM every time its state
changes. Not the same as the *Watch page*.

**Watch page**: the community's public page at `/live`. You don't need to sign
in, and it shows each stage's stream, what's on now and what's up next. See [The
Watch page](/help/on-air#the-watch-page).

**Withdraw**: taking yourself off something you signed up for, like a crew slot,
a tournament while its signups are open, or a change request staff haven't
answered yet.

## Match states

These are the five states a match moves through, and the ones the *State* filter
picks from. The boards' *Status* column names some of them by what happens next.
Started shows as *Live*, a Scheduled match past its time shows as *Late*, and on
Admin › Schedule and Proctor Station a Finished match shows as *To confirm* or
*Flagged*.

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| {state:Scheduled} | A time is booked. |
| {state:Checked In} | Players are seated and the match is checked in. |
| {state:Started} | Under way. |
| {state:Finished} | A result is recorded but isn't official yet. |
| {state:Confirmed} | Staff have confirmed the result, so it's official. |
