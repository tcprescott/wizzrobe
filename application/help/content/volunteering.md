Volunteering means working shifts that keep the event running. Crew is
different: you sign up for [crew](/help/crew) match by match.

Your shifts are on Home → My Schedule, and everything else is under Volunteer.
If you can't see Volunteer, you haven't been given a volunteer role yet, so ask a
coordinator. Some positions give you the role they need (Proctor, say, or
Check-in Desk) once a coordinator puts you on one of their shifts, so a new tab
can show up after you're scheduled.

> Proctoring has its own tab and its own procedure. If you're running a room,
> read [Running matches as a proctor](/help/proctor).

## Say when you're free

:::snippet volunteer-availability
My Availability, on the Volunteer tab, is where you say when you can work. Each
window you add gets one of three answers:

| Window | Means |
|---|---|
| Preferred | You'd like this slot. The auto-scheduler tries these first. |
| Available | You can work it. Used after everyone's preferred windows. |
| Unavailable | You can't work it. The auto-scheduler won't put you here. |

Your windows are grouped into a card per day. **Add a day** starts a new card,
**Add window** adds another window to that day, and nothing's saved until you
press **Save availability**. When two windows overlap, Unavailable beats
Preferred, and Preferred beats Available.

Leaving your schedule blank means you haven't said anything, which isn't the
same as unavailable. But by default the auto-scheduler only places people who
entered a window, so a blank schedule usually gets passed over. A coordinator
can let it fill outside stated availability, and then you're considered last,
behind everyone who said something.

So an Unavailable window is the only way to rule a time out, and entering your
windows is how you get the shifts you want.
:::

> The availability editor on Home works the other way round. This one is when
> you can work, and a shift only reaches you inside a window you entered. The
> one on Home is when you can't play: you count as free for the whole event and
> block out the times you aren't. Filling in one doesn't fill in the other.

## How shifts reach you

A coordinator builds the schedule, by hand or with the auto-scheduler, which
matches people to shifts using everyone's availability. The coordinator always
has the final say on what it drafts. A drafted shift isn't yours yet. You won't
hear about it, and it won't show on My Schedule, until the coordinator publishes
it.

The auto-scheduler won't put you on two shifts at once, and it sticks to a cap on
how many hours anyone works. If a coordinator has recorded which positions
you're qualified for, it keeps you to those and puts you ahead of unqualified
people for them.

Once a shift is yours, the bot sends you a Discord DM:

| DM | When | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Volunteer shift assigned | A coordinator puts you on a shift. | Tap **Acknowledge** to say you'll cover it. |
| Volunteer shift reminder | Before it starts (an hour, unless your community set a different lead). | Nothing, if you're coming. If you can't, its **Can't make it** button opens that shift's give-back dialog. |
| Your shift is starting | When the shift starts, unless you've already said you're here. | Tap **I'm here** once you're at your post. **Tell staff I'm late** asks how late (about 10, about 20, or 30 minutes or more) and tells your coordinator, and you keep the shift. **I can't make it** asks for an optional reason and gives the shift back. |
| Volunteer shift moved | Its start or end time changes. | Tap **Acknowledge** again. Saying yes to the old time doesn't count for the new one. |
| Volunteer shift removed | A coordinator takes you off it. | Nothing. It's gone from My Schedule. |

If your DMs are off, you still have the shift. Check My Schedule.

## Your shifts

:::snippet my-shifts
Your shifts are on Home → My Schedule under Your volunteer shifts, alongside
your matches and crew slots. They're grouped by day, soonest first, in your
local time. Each row shows the position and the shift's label, when it runs, and
where it stands: *Needs your OK*, *Acknowledged*, *On now* or *Arrived*.

The line under the heading is your hours. It counts everything you're scheduled
for this event, past and upcoming, with overlapping shifts counted once. If your
community comps volunteers by hours worked, it also says which level you've
reached and how many more hours get you to the next one.

A row's button is **Acknowledge** until you press it, which does the same thing
as the button in the DM. From 30 minutes before a shift until it ends, the button
becomes **I'm here**, and a banner with the same button shows at the top of
every page. Tap it when you reach your post so your coordinator knows you've
arrived. The row then says *Arrived* with the time.

The row's ⋮ menu has the rest.

- **Shift details** shows what the position involves, any For this shift notes
  from the coordinator, and who else is on the slot.
- **Undo check-in** is there if you tapped **I'm here** by mistake. It works
  until the shift ends, unless a coordinator checked you in.
- **Can't make it…** gives the shift back.

If your coordinator doesn't need you after all, they can call your shift off.
You get a DM, the row says *Called off*, and there's nothing to check in to.
The shift still counts as fully worked toward your hours.

Shifts that have ended fold into **Finished shifts** at the bottom of the
section. Tap it to see them, newest first.
:::

## Full Schedule

:::snippet full-schedule
Full Schedule shows every role's shifts for one day, so you can see who's at
which station and when. Pick a day along the top, and the page remembers it next
time.

Each role is a column, with the hours down the side. A shift is a block with its
times and the names on it, and the blocks outlined in the accent colour are
yours. During the event a line marks the current time. Shifts the coordinator is
still drafting don't show until they're sent out. A block that says *Nobody yet*
has no one on it.

On a phone the hour column stays put while you swipe sideways through the roles.
If a block has more names than fit, tap **+N more** for the full list.

**Columns** picks which roles you see and in what order. Untick a role to hide
it, and drag a row (or use the arrows) to move it. Your choice is saved to your
account, so it follows you to every device. A role added later shows up at the
end, and a dot on **Columns** means you've changed something.
:::

## Giving a shift back

:::snippet shift-release
If you can't work a shift, give it back instead of not showing up. Press
**Can't make it…** in the shift's ⋮ menu on My Schedule, or **Can't make it** in
the reminder DM. Your coordinator hears about it right away and can look for
cover.

You can add a reason if you like, and your coordinator will see it.

Close to the start time, the app warns you that it's late notice. Your
coordinator will still know, but there may not be time to replace you, so give
it back as early as you can.

The button goes away once you're checked in or the shift has ended. After that,
talk to your coordinator.
:::
