Attendees can add one emergency contact on their Profile.

## Opening a contact

Go to Admin → Members, find the person, and choose **⋮ → Emergency contact…**.
You'll see their contact's name, relationship and phone, with **Call** (on a
phone) and **Copy number**.

Opening it is logged right away with your name, the person and the time, even if
they haven't added a contact. The attendee sees on their Profile who last opened
it and when. So only open it when you need to call someone, not to check whether
one's on file.

## What stays private

There's no list or export of contacts, and they're never sent anywhere outside
the app.
