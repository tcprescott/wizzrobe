If you need something arranged so you can take part, ask from your Profile. You
don't have to track down a staff member at the event. Staff see every request in
one place and can sort it out before you arrive.

## Making a request

:::snippet ada-request
Open Profile and find the ADA accommodation card. Tick **I'd like to request an
ADA accommodation** and staff are told straight away.

Details is optional. Say what would help, like step-free access to the stage, a
seat near an exit, or extra time between matches. It saves as you type.

Only this community's staff can read what you write. Once your request is
arranged, proctors see an "ADA:" line with your name on their match run sheets,
plus a button in the match's Ready to play? checklist telling them to check with
staff. They don't see your details. We can't promise notes stay private, so
leave out anything sensitive and tell staff directly.
:::

![The ADA accommodation card on Profile with the I'd like to request an ADA accommodation box ticked and marked 1, the Details box filled in and marked 2, and an Arranged status](/static/help/accessibility/ada-card-phone.webp "An arranged request: the box you tick (1) and your details (2).")

A request only counts in the community you made it in. If you play in more than
one, ask in each.

## What the status means

Once you've asked, the card shows where staff are with it, next to *Staff
status*:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| {chip:pending:New} | Staff haven't looked at it yet. |
| {chip:live:Acknowledged} | Staff have read it and are working on it. |
| {chip:ok:Arranged} | It's in place. Proctors see the "ADA:" line on your matches. |
| {chip:neutral:Withdrawn} | You took it back. Staff see this status. Your own card just shows the box unticked. |

If you edit your details after a request is arranged, staff are told. What they
arranged stays in place while they check the change. If you edit one that's only
{chip:live:Acknowledged}, it goes back to {chip:pending:New} so staff read it
again.

## Changing your mind

Untick the box to withdraw. It asks first. **Withdraw request** clears your
details and staff see it as withdrawn. **Keep request** leaves it alone. You can
tick the box again any time, and staff will look at it from the start.
