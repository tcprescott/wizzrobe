Wizzrobe reaches you by Discord DM, and on any phone or browser you turn on under
Profile → Notifications. If your community has switched them on, email and
Telegram can get a copy of every DM too. There's no SMS.

## What you get DMed

| You are | You hear about |
|---|---|
| A player | Being scheduled, rescheduled or cancelled; being taken off a match; your match being confirmed; the answer to a change request you sent |
| A player whose opponent asked to change a match | Their request, with an **Agree** button |
| Crew | Your signup being approved, and approval being withdrawn |
| A volunteer | Being assigned a shift, and a reminder before it starts |
| Watching a match | Every state change on it |
| Someone who tapped **Remind me** or **RSVP** on a community event | A reminder shortly before it starts, and word if it moves or is cancelled |

## The buttons on a DM

:::snippet dm-buttons
DMs come with buttons, so you can answer without opening the site.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Acknowledge** | On a match DM, it tells staff you've seen the time. On a crew approval DM, it confirms you can cover the slot. |
| **Unwatch** | Stops the updates for that match. |
| **Agree** | On a reschedule request DM, it tells staff the change works for you too. Staff still make the decision. |
| A link button, like **Pick a time**, **Open your seed** or **View your match** | Opens the site right where you act on it: the time picker, your seed, your match. |

A button in Discord does exactly what the same control on the site does. Use
whichever one's in front of you.
:::

You can only start watching a match from the site, with the bell at the end of
its line on the schedule. Discord can stop it but can't start it.

## When DMs don't arrive

It's nearly always one of these four:

1. Discord DMs is switched off under Where it goes on your Profile. The page
   warns you when nothing at all can reach you, but not when only Discord is
   off.
2. Your Discord privacy settings block DMs from server members. Open the
   community's server settings in Discord and allow direct messages.
3. You've blocked the bot, or you don't share a server with it. Rejoin the
   community's server.
4. You're signed in with a different Discord account from the one you actually
   use. Check which one it is.

Once you fix it, DMs start again from the next thing that happens. The ones you
missed aren't resent, so check the schedule board for anything you might have
missed.

## Where your settings live

It's all on the Profile tab, under Notifications.

:::snippet notification-settings
Where it goes has a switch for each way Wizzrobe can reach you.

- **Discord DMs** turns the Discord copy on or off.
- **This phone**, **This tablet** or **This browser** turns on pop-up
  notifications on the device you're holding. Other devices you've turned on are
  listed below it, each with **Remove**.
- **Email**, if your community offers it, sends a copy of every DM to your
  inbox. **Add email** lets you use your Discord account's address in one tap,
  or type a different one and confirm it with a 6-digit code. Every email has a
  one-click unsubscribe link.
- **Telegram**, if your community offers it, sends the same copy as a Telegram
  message. **Connect** opens a chat with the Wizzrobe bot. Press Start and
  you're done, with no phone number needed. Send /stop in that chat to turn it
  off.

**Send a test…** sends a test message to the places you pick, so you can check
each one really reaches you. You can send 3 tests every 10 minutes.

Each switch works on its own, so you can turn Discord DMs off and still get
notifications on your phone. If you turn everything off, the page warns you,
because nothing can reach you.

What you hear about starts with how much you want to hear. Pick one of three:

| Pick | What pings you |
|---|---|
| Everything | Every message your community sends, the way it sends it |
| Only what needs me | Messages where you have something to do, like acknowledging a match, picking a time or taking a shift. The rest still arrive, just silently |
| Quiet | Only what can't wait: getting checked in, stage calls, race rooms, shift starts, cancellations. Nothing about matches you watch or follow |

Each one shows how many messages would ping, arrive silently or stay off.
Open **Customize** to change a single message: tap its chip and pick Off,
Silent or Ping. **Reset to preset…** undoes those changes and keeps your pick.

Some messages are off unless you turn them on, like hearing that your opponent
acknowledged, that you've been given a station, or how your bracket match left
you. They say "Off unless you turn it on" under Customize.

Your community decides what it sends, and you can only make things quieter. If
it sends a message silently you can't make it ping, and one it switched off
shows **Off here**.

Below that, Tournaments you follow adds other people's matches. Pick a level
for each tournament:

| Level | You hear about |
|---|---|
| Not following | Nothing extra from that tournament |
| Streamed matches | Its streamed matches being scheduled |
| Streamed, plus possible streams | The above, plus matches that *might* be streamed |
| Every match | Every match it schedules |

You don't have to be playing in a tournament to follow it, and your own matches
reach you either way.
:::

## Turning it down

If one tournament is too noisy, set it to Not following. If it's one kind of
message, switch it to Silent or Off under Customize. Either beats blocking the
bot. Blocking the bot also cuts off the DMs you do want, like hearing that your
match moved or that you've been approved for a slot.
