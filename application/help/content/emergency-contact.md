If you're hurt or unwell at an event, staff can call someone for you. They just
need to know who.

## Adding a contact

:::snippet emergency-contact
Open Profile and find the Emergency contact card. Fill in their Name and Phone.
Relationship is optional. It saves once both the name and phone are in.

Only this community's staff can open it, one person at a time, and every time
they do it goes in the audit log. The card shows you who last opened it and
when. Other communities you belong to never see it, so add one in each community
where you want it.

**Remove contact** deletes it straight away. It's also deleted if you're removed
from the community.
:::
