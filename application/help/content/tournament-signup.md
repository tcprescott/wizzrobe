Signing up puts you in a tournament's player pool. Staff can only schedule you
into its matches once you're in the pool.

## Signing up

The Tournaments tab lists every tournament your community is running. Each card
shows its dates, a **Rules** link if staff have posted them, how many people have
signed up (tap the count to see who), and a **Sign up** button while signups are
open. Some also have Randomizer notes, which are staff's notes on the settings
its seeds use.

:::snippet tournament-signup
Sign up on the Tournaments tab to join a tournament's player pool. Staff schedule
matches from that pool, so nothing gets scheduled for you until you're in it.
Following a tournament for match alerts is a separate setting on your Profile.
You can do either one without the other.
:::

Cards are grouped by what you can do with them. Tournaments taking signups come
first, then ones you're already in, then ones that are announced but not open
yet, then closed ones.

## When signups close

Staff set a signup window for each tournament, and the card tells you what's
next: when signups open, or when they close. Some tournaments have no window and
stay open until staff make them inactive.

Once the window shuts, the Sign up button goes away. Staff can still add you at
any point, so ask them if you want in.

## Backing out

Tap **Withdraw** on the same card while signups are still open. It asks first.
**Stay in** keeps your place and **Withdraw** takes you out. After signups close,
the card says *Signups have closed — ask staff if you need to drop out*. By then
it's staff's call, because a bracket may already have been seeded from the
roster.

## Brackets on Challonge

Some tournaments run their bracket on Challonge. Those cards have a Challonge
tag, and **Sign up** adds you to the Challonge bracket itself, using the
Challonge account linked on your Profile. Before anything is sent, a short sheet
shows the bracket and the Challonge name you'll join as. Challonge then emails
that account an invite to confirm the entry is yours.

Haven't linked Challonge yet? The button reads **Link Challonge to sign up**. It
takes you through Challonge's sign-in and brings you back to the card to finish.

You can **Withdraw** from a Challonge bracket while signups are open and the
bracket hasn't started. Once it starts, Challonge locks the roster, so the card
says *Bracket started* and dropping out is up to staff.

If staff added you on Challonge directly, link your Challonge account under
Connected accounts on your Profile and the card will show you as signed up. Your
bracket matches then appear on My Schedule, ready for you to pick a time.
