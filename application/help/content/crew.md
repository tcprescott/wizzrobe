Crew covers the two jobs behind a streamed match. Commentators talk over the
match on stream. Trackers run the item and progress tracking viewers see next to
the game, the overlay showing what each player has found and how far along they
are.

Both work the same way: sign up, get approved, confirm, turn up. Anyone in the
community can do it, and you don't need a volunteer role.

## Signing up

:::snippet crew-signup
Open Event, stay on the Schedule view, and turn on **Needs crew**. That hides
every match that's already covered or under way, which on a busy day is most of
the board. Then tap **Sign up** in the match's Commentators or Trackers column.
On a phone each role sits under the match name with its own button, **Sign up as
commentator** or **Sign up as tracker**.

There's no confirm step. If you tapped the wrong one, **Withdraw** is in the
same spot.

Signing up tells staff you're available for that slot. A staff member decides
who actually gets it.

Every streamed match has commentary. Some tournaments don't use trackers, and
for those the Trackers column is empty on purpose.
:::

![A match on the board on a phone: Racer Five vs Racer Six on Stage 3, Commentary 0/1 and Tracking 0/1 both marked needs crew, with Sign up as commentator marked 1 and Sign up as tracker marked 2](/static/help/crew/signup-phone.webp "A match that still needs crew: tap **Sign up as commentator** (1) or **Sign up as tracker** (2).")

If you can't sign up, the **Sign up** button is greyed out with the reason next
to it.

- *You're playing* means it's your own match, and you can't crew your own game.
- *Signups closed* means the match has started. Signups close when a proctor
  starts it. A match that's only checked in is still open, since that's exactly
  when a stream finds out it's short a commentator.

If you've already signed up for that role on that match, you'll see
**Withdraw** instead.

## Signing up before a match is booked

Want the final while the semifinals haven't been played? Open **Not scheduled
yet** at the bottom of Event › Schedule. It lists every bracket match nobody has
booked a time for, with the sides it's still waiting on ("Andy or Benny",
"Winner of C", "TBD"). Sign up there the same way.

Your signup waits on the match. When it gets booked, your signup moves onto the
booking, approved or not, and the match shows up on your list with its time.
Staff can approve you before then. If you turn out to be busy at the time it's
booked for, the staff who approve crew get told so they can sort it out.

You won't get a new DM when it's booked. As with every match, you hear about it
once it's on a stage. If you end up playing in it, your crew signup is dropped.

## Signing up from Discord

If you follow a tournament, the DM announcing each new match has **Sign up as
Commentator** and **Sign up as Tracker** buttons. They do the same thing as
**Sign up** on the board. The DM's **View the schedule** button opens the board
if you want to see who's playing first.

You pick which tournaments DM you, and how much, under Profile → Notifications
→ Tournaments you follow.
[Notifications and Discord DMs](/help/notifications#where-your-settings-live)
explains the levels. Most crew want *Streamed, plus possible streams*, which
covers matches with a stage and the ones staff are thinking about streaming.

## Getting approved

:::snippet crew-approval
Staff have the final say on every crew slot. A staff member, the tournament's
admin or its crew coordinator decides who covers each one. Signing up puts your
name forward but doesn't reserve anything.

It isn't first come, first served. Staff can see what else you're doing at that
time, including matches you're playing in, and a clash is the usual reason a
signup gets passed over.

Staff get a Discord DM as soon as you sign up. When they approve you, you get a
DM too, with an **Acknowledge** button and a **View your crew slot** button that
opens your slot on My Schedule.

Staff can also put you on a match you never signed up for. You get the same DM,
and the slot shows up on My Schedule ready to confirm.

They can withdraw an approval, right up to the day of the match, if plans for
the stream change or someone better suited frees up. You'll get a DM, your
confirmation is cleared, and you go back to waiting for approval. If they take
you off the match completely, it disappears from your list. Neither is a
judgement on you.
:::

## Confirming

:::snippet crew-confirm
Once you're approved, you still need to confirm you can cover the slot. Until
you do, staff can't tell whether you've seen the approval.

You can confirm in any of these places, and they all do the same thing:

- **Confirm**, next to **Withdraw** in your role's column on My Schedule
- the same **Confirm** on the schedule board
- **Acknowledge**, on the DM you were sent
:::

## My Schedule

Crew you signed up for, on Home's My Schedule tab, is the schedule board cut
down to the matches you're crewing. If you aren't playing in any tournament it's
at the top of the tab. If you are, it comes after your own matches. Each line
looks the same as on the schedule, with your **Withdraw** (and **Confirm**, once
you're approved) in your role's column, so the names tell you where you stand:

:::snippet crew-status
| Beside your name | What it means |
|---|---|
| A clock, *waiting for approval* | You've signed up. Staff haven't decided yet. |
| A tick, with **Confirm** in your tray | You have the slot. Confirm that you can still cover it. |
| A tick, and no **Confirm** | You have the slot and you've confirmed. Nothing left to do. |

Finished matches drop off. Add *Finished* and *Confirmed* to the State filter to
see the ones you've already worked.
:::

## Reading the crew columns

The board has a Commentators column and a Trackers column. Each starts with
approved crew against what the match needs, so "1/2" means one approved
commentator out of two. People still waiting for approval don't count, so a
match can have three signups and still say "needs crew". **Needs crew** uses the
same rule to decide whether a match is covered. Crew only works matches on a
stage, so a match with no stage yet just shows how many have signed up and
never says "needs crew". On a phone, each card names the role instead
("Commentary 1/2").

Your own **Sign up** or **Withdraw** sits in a shaded box under the count, in
the same place on every match. When there's nothing you can do, the button is
greyed out with the reason, like "Signups closed" or "You're playing".

The names come after that. A tick means approved, and a clock with "waiting for
approval" means not yet. Your own name has "(you)" after it. "No signups yet"
means nobody's asked for that slot.

## Withdrawing

:::snippet crew-withdraw
**Withdraw** takes you off a slot. You can do it from My Schedule or the
schedule board, before or after approval, even once the match has started.

Withdrawing before anyone's approved you costs nobody anything. Withdrawing from
a slot you were approved for means staff have to find cover, so they're told
right away, and the app warns you about that before you confirm.

If you'd already confirmed, withdrawing clears that too. Do it as early as you
can.
:::

## If nobody approves you

Signups wait until staff get to them, which at a busy event might be shortly
before the match. If a match you signed up for has started and you never heard
back, the slot went to someone else. You can withdraw the old signup or leave
it. Either way it drops off your list once the match ends.

## On the day

Where to be and when, who runs the countdown and how a stage works are up to
the event, not the app. If your community publishes them, they're under Event
Guide in the sidebar.
