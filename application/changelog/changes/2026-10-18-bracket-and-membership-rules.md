### Improved

- A tournament's admins can now sync its Challonge bracket, the same as start.gg. Linking and unlinking a bracket on either site stays with staff.
- Reports now count a match as finished when it has a finish time, even without a recorded start, so its numbers agree with Insights.

### Fixed

- Platform admins now join a community before sending feedback or posting community events, like everyone else.
- A community a platform admin has deactivated no longer gets bracket syncs, seed rolls or reminders until it's switched back on.
