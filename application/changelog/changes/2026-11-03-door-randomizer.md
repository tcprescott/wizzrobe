### New

- Tournaments can roll ALttP Door Randomizer seeds from a preset. The seed link opens a page in the app where you pick your Japanese 1.0 ROM once, then tap Download ROM. Your ROM never leaves your device.
- Customize on that page sets your sprite (any from alttpr.com), heart colour and beep, menu speed, quickswap, music, palettes and reduced flashing, and remembers them for your next seed.
