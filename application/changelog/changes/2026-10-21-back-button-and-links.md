### Improved

- Back and Forward now step through what you switched on a page: tabs, My Schedule's List and Timeline, the Event board's views, the day on a timeline or shift grid, a Settings page, the Not booked yet board and the match details sheet. The address bar and the browser tab's title follow along, so a reload or a copied link opens exactly the view you were on. Filters like the check-in desk's chips and the feedback status stay in the address without adding Back steps.
- Sections, matches and shifts can be linked to directly: add `#crew-you-signed-up-for`, `#match-123` or `#shift-45` to a page's address and it scrolls there once the page has loaded.
- Every section heading has a link icon (it shows when you point at the heading on a computer, and stays on a phone) that copies a link straight to that section. Your matches and your volunteer shifts have **Copy link** in their ⋮ menu too.
