### New

- Sixteen new messages you can turn on under Profile → Notifications → Customize. They're off for everyone until you pick them. Players can hear when an opponent acknowledges a match, the result of their match once staff confirm it, when they're given a station, when staff add or remove them from a tournament, when an opponent withdraws a request, and when a qualifier opens or closes. Staff and tournament admins can hear when a match is flagged for review, a seed fails to roll, a player withdraws, a match is offered for stream, or a volunteer arrives. Anyone can hear results and bracket starts in tournaments they follow, and when a new community event is listed.
