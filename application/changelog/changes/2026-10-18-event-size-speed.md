### Improved

- Schedule boards load about twice as fast with a full event's worth of matches. Home → Event schedule and Proctor Station now take half the server time they did with 1,300 matches, and picking **Today** or **Tomorrow** on a board only loads that day's matches.
- Admin → Match Schedule opens on **Today**. Pick **All dates** in the Day filter to see the whole event.
- Admin → Reports → Insights and the engagement telemetry totals stay quick after a year of history; the telemetry totals took over ten seconds on a busy community.
- Engagement telemetry older than 13 months is deleted once a day, so the reports keep this year's event and last year's.
