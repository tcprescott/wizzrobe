### New

- Players can release their own seed. Turn on "Players release their own seed" under a tournament's Seeds & randomizer settings, and once a proctor checks a match in, each player taps Ready for seed on My Schedule or on their check-in DM. The seed rolls and goes to everyone the moment the last player is ready, and anyone can take Ready back until then. Proctors and staff can still roll it whenever they like, and their boards show "1 of 2 ready" on the seed line.
