### New

- An archived event is read-only: its pages say so under the title, its add buttons and ⋮ menus are gone, and anything that tries to change it is turned away. Staff can unarchive it from the same notice.
- Archiving an event closes its open race rooms, drops seed rolls still waiting, and takes back the Discord roles its rules gave.
- Emergency contacts and accommodation requests for an archived event are deleted 30 days after it was archived.

### Fixed

- The Match Schedule's "Needs you" list and "Not booked yet" view only show the event you're looking at.
