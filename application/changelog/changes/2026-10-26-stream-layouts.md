### New

- Stream layouts: staff and Broadcast Techs can design each event's broadcast overlay under Admin → Stream Layouts (start from a stock layout on a plain colour, with the same box positions as the SGL SpeedGaming and FFR layouts, or upload your own art, then place player names, round, commentators, seed code, up next and more on it). Each stage gets an OBS link that follows the match on that stage, and you can download ready-made OBS scenes with every video hole wired to a shared feed, and a match can be pinned from its match sheet. Communities turn it on under Admin → Features once it's made available.
