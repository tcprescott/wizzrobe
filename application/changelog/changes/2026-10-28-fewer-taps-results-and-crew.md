### Improved

- Reporting a result is one tap: a live two-player match shows both names under "Who won?" on the board and in the match, and a short Undo replaces the confirm steps. A result staff or the tournament admin records is confirmed in the same tap.
- Approving a commentator, tracker or broadcaster is one tap with Undo, and their DM waits until Undo runs out. Confirming a proctor's result is one tap with Undo too.

### Fixed

- The board's Approve button no longer resets to Approve when another match changes, which made the second tap look like it did nothing.
