### Improved

- Sending a survey with a required question left blank now lists what's missing at the top, marks those parts, and jumps straight to each question.
- The survey builder shows how long most people will take beside the longest path, plus a Checks list of things worth a second look, like a rating that asks two things at once or a part nobody will see.
- The survey preview starts with a table of what each kind of person sees, and greys out what they won't see, with the reason.
- A duplicated survey's results show last time's numbers under each carried question.
