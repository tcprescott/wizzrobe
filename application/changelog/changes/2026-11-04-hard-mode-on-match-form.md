### Improved

- Hard mode is now a question on the match form: each player gets a private "Play Hard mode?" under Before your match, and a seed rule rolls the harder preset when everyone says Yes. Tournaments that had a harder preset were moved over with their players' answers.
- Staff can force any preset for the tournament's randomizer on a single match, not just the standard or harder one. The tournament's separate Harder Preset setting is gone.
- The old site's Harder settings button and the Discord opt-in buttons now point players to the match's questions.
