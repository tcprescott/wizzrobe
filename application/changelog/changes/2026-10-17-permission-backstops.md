### Security

- Reports, the volunteer hours roster, Triforce text moderation, race room controls, player availability, system settings and the Service Health board now check who's asking themselves, so a new page or connector tool can't skip the check.
- Tournament admin, crew coordinator and qualifier reviewer grants now refuse accounts from outside the community.

- Sync admins can list open race rooms over the API and connector, matching the Racetime tab.
