### New

- Challonge and start.gg tournaments have a Round schedule (Admin → Tournaments → ⋮ → Round schedule…). It lists the group stage and each elimination round down to the final, and staff set when each one opens and closes for booking, plus optional daily hours like "6 pm to 11 pm". Rounds can be planned before Challonge creates them.
- Players can only book or reschedule a match inside its round's window, and Suggest a time only offers times that fit. My Schedule shows each matchup's window under its round. Staff get a warning and can book outside it anyway. Native bracket round times now work the same way.
