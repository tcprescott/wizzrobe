### New
- Surveys can ask an agree scale: several statements, each answered from Strongly disagree to Strongly agree. It's a grid on a computer and one card per statement on a phone, and results show a bar per statement.
- Staff can press Suggest questions on a draft survey section to get a few AI-drafted questions built from what happened at the event. The community uses its own Claude or OpenAI-compatible key under Settings → Integrations, nothing is sent until someone presses the button, and no names or anything people typed are ever sent.
- Survey choice questions can add "Other" with a write-in box, a "None of these" answer that clears the other ticks, and a limit on how many people pick. Comments can be switched to a long answer with a character count.
- Survey sections can have a short description, and commentators, trackers and broadcast crew can each get their own section.
- Drag questions by their handle to reorder a survey, including into another section. On a phone, press and hold the handle first.

### Improved
- 1–5 and 0–10 survey questions are now sliders, so they fit on a phone screen.
- Surveys only count someone as crew if the match they crewed was on a stage.
- The anonymous option now says plainly that what you write may still identify you.
