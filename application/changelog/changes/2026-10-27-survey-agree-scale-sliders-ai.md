### New
- Surveys can ask an agree scale: several statements, each answered from Strongly disagree to Strongly agree. It's a grid on a computer and one card per statement on a phone, and results show a bar per statement.
- Staff can press Suggest questions on a draft survey section to get a few AI-drafted questions built from what happened at the event. The community uses its own Claude or OpenAI-compatible key under Settings → Integrations, nothing is sent until someone presses the button, and no names or anything people typed are ever sent.

### Improved
- 1–5 and 0–10 survey questions are now sliders, so they fit on a phone screen.
