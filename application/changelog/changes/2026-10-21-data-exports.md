### New

- Admin → Reports → Exports downloads your tournaments as a ZIP of participants and matches spreadsheets plus one JSON file: each player's bracket status, seed, placement and win-loss, and every match with its times and results. Staff can export any tournament; a tournament admin, the ones they run.
- Every export you make, including volunteer data and table CSVs, is kept for 3 days under Your exports on that page, so a download you missed is still there.
