### New

- Staff and preset managers can host ROM patches under Admin → Patches in the app. Upload a BPS, IPS or UPS file and share its link: players pick their own ROM once and download the patched game. Their ROM never leaves their device.
- Anyone can patch a ROM with a BPS, IPS or UPS file they already have at the new ROM patcher page. A ROM you've used before is picked for you when it matches.
