### Fixed

- A booked match that start.gg or Challonge closed with no winner (a double DQ, or a dead pool match) no longer shows "Someone won" with a Record result button that fails. The Match Schedule card now says it was closed with no winner and offers Call off.
- Matching a start.gg or Challonge entrant to a different member now moves that entrant's booked matches that haven't started yet onto the new member, who gets the usual "please acknowledge" DM. Before, the matches kept the old account, so results couldn't go back to the bracket site.
