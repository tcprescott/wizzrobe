### Improved
- Survey results read in the survey's own sections, one line per question with a bar and its key number, so a big survey fits on a few screens instead of dozens.
- A section asked once per tournament or volunteer position is a comparison table, and a tournament or position well below the rest is flagged.
- Comments open in a side sheet with search and a chip per tournament or position.
- Filter the whole results page to one tournament's players, one volunteer position, crew or people who checked in. Results also show the response rate for players, volunteers and crew.
