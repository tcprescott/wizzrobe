### Fixed

- Moving a match to a new time now sends a fresh "you're on stage" reminder for the new slot. Before, a match moved after its reminder went out got none.
- Turning a feature on or off under Admin → Features now reaches reminders and syncs on their next run, rather than after the app restarts.
- The volunteer export's Assigned Hours counts overlapping shifts once, so it matches the Hours roster.
- Check-in desk volunteers can no longer resend the check-in DM for an event that's closed.
- Volunteers can't give back a shift that's still a draft.
- Deactivated staff accounts no longer get join request, ADA, community event or volunteer alert DMs.
