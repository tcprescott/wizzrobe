### Improved

- The crew signup DM tournament admins and crew coordinators get now updates itself: it says when the volunteer withdraws, when someone approves them ("You" on your own copy) or when staff remove the signup, so nobody opens the board for a signup that's already gone.
- Commentator and tracker signups are now separate rows in Admin → Notifications and Profile → Notifications, and so are "Commentator withdrew" and "Tracker withdrew". New signup DMs arrive silently by default. Any setting you'd already changed carries over to both.
