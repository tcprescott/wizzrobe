### New

- Staff can decide a match by forfeit. Open the match, tap ⋮ › Forfeit…, pick who's out and why (no-show, withdrew or disqualified), and the match is confirmed in one step. The bracket moves on, the players get one DM instead of four, and the board shows it as Forfeit.
- Removing a player from a tournament's Players… list now drops them properly. You pick once whether their open matches go to their opponents by forfeit or get called off, and if the tournament runs on Challonge they come out of the bracket there too, so the next sync doesn't add them back.
