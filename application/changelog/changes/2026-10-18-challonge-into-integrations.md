### Improved

- The Admin → Challonge page is gone. The Challonge sign-in is now the first card on Settings → Integrations, and each tournament's bracket is managed from its ⋮ on Admin → Tournaments: sync, the next stage step, entrants and unlink. A new Bracket column shows the site, stage and last sync, and "Link a bracket…" takes a Challonge or start.gg link.
