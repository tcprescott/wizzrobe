### New

- Times now show on the clock your browser prefers: 9:30 PM if it uses 12-hour time, 21:30 if it uses 24-hour, and 9:30 PM when it doesn't say. If the guess is wrong, pick 12-hour or 24-hour under Profile → Time format, next to Timezone. Discord DMs follow your own Discord setting; email, Telegram and phone notifications, CSV exports and the Watch page use 12-hour.
