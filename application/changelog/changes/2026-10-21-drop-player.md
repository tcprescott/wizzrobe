### New

- Removing a player from a tournament's Players… list now drops them properly. Pick why (no-show, withdrew or disqualified) and choose once whether their open matches go to their opponents by forfeit or get called off. Forfeited matches are confirmed straight away, the bracket moves on, and the board shows them as Forfeit. If the tournament runs on Challonge they come out of the bracket there too, so the next sync doesn't add them back.
