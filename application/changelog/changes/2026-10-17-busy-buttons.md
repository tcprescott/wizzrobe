### Improved

- Buttons that wait on another site, like rolling seeds, opening a racetime room, syncing Challonge, start.gg or SpeedGaming, or publishing volunteer shifts, now say what they're doing ("Syncing…", "Rolling 7 of 25…") and count the seconds on long jobs. They can't be pressed twice while they work, so a double tap no longer opens two race rooms or rolls a second batch of seeds.
- Confirm boxes for slow actions stay open until the work is done instead of closing onto a page that looked like nothing happened.
