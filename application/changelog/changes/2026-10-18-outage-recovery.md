### Fixed

- When Discord, Challonge, start.gg, racetime or a randomizer is down, buttons now answer with what went wrong (for example "Challonge isn't answering right now") instead of spinning for minutes or doing nothing.
- A half-working Challonge, start.gg or SpeedGaming no longer wipes a synced bracket, its players or its schedule. The sync refuses and nothing changes.
- Stage, volunteer and event reminders that fall due while the Discord bot is reconnecting are sent once it's back, instead of being lost.
- A phone that locks or loses Wi-Fi for a few seconds keeps its page and any half-typed dialog, instead of reloading.
