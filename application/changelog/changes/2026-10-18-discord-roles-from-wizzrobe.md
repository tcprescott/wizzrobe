### New

- Wizzrobe can hand out roles in your Discord server: one for everyone enrolled in a tournament, one for people in a match right now, one per tournament, and one each for VIP, Base and Day pass badge holders. Pick the roles under Admin → Settings → Discord → Discord Roles from Wizzrobe. It's off until a platform admin makes it available and staff turn it on under Features. Wizzrobe only takes back roles it gave, and it won't let you pick a role the bot can't hand out.
