### New

- Staff decide how every message Wizzrobe sends arrives (ping, silent or off) for each kind of recipient, under Admin → Notifications. On Profile → Notifications you pick Everything, Only what needs me or Quiet, adjust any single message, and can turn on sixteen extra ones that are off by default.
- Messages sent to you, on Profile → Notifications, lists every message Wizzrobe sent you. The line at the bottom of each notification opens that exact message so you can change how you get it.
- Staff can see every DM sent in the community and whether it arrived, under Admin → Reports → Sent messages, and clicking a name on Admin → Members opens everything about that person, including how messages reach them.
- Prize pools follow sglive.speedgaming.org every 15 minutes and show beside each tournament on the event info list and the Watch page.
- Best-of-3 and best-of-5 sets on Challonge and start.gg are booked as one match per game, and the set result goes to the bracket with its game score.
- Staff can book a bracket match before its players are known ("Andy or Benny vs Cleo or Dash"), or create a placeholder match with just a title to hold a slot.
- Players can tell staff they're running late from My Schedule or the stage reminder DM. The admin on duty hears about it; the opponent doesn't.
- Dropping a player from a tournament forfeits or calls off their open matches in one go, and takes them off the Challonge bracket too.
- Admin → Reports → Exports downloads a tournament's players and matches, and keeps every export for 3 days.
- Challonge brackets can sync on a schedule, and a match someone already reported on Challonge or start.gg shows under Needs you to record in one tap.

### Improved

- The Watch page shows each stage as a tile with a live picture of its stream, and a bell on any upcoming match DMs you before it goes on stage. Staff can send signed-out viewers to a lightweight version so a crowd can't slow the app down.
- Back and Forward step through tabs and views, a copied link opens the exact view you were on, and every section heading, match and shift has a link you can copy.
- Match boards can fold any group (Finished starts folded), reset filters in one tap and approve crew in place. Big races show three names and "+N more" and finish without picking a winner, and Broadcast Techs get Proctor Station for stage matches.
- My Schedule only shows what applies to you, and your Timeline adds community events and the crew slots still waiting on a stage.
- The Capacity Forecast splits off-stream from on-stream players, keeps late matches on the clock, and leaves out tournaments played outside the tournament room.
- Crew hear about a match only once it's on a stage, and the signup DM staff get updates itself when someone approves or withdraws the signup.
- The side menu is bigger on a phone, with Home, Volunteer and Admin in a strip at the top that swaps the page list without leaving your page. Plenty of other screens got tidier on a phone too.
- The event guide opens inside the app and has a Community events page, and staff can post a community event on another member's behalf.

### Fixed

- On iPhone, links and buttons work on the first tap in the Home Screen app, turning on notifications no longer crashes Safari (Help now shows how to add Wizzrobe to the Home Screen), the bottom bar stays put, and older Safari gets an update message instead of a blank page.
- The brief whole-app freezes during the event are much shorter, busy pages no longer stall everyone while they load, and schedules stop jumping back to page 1.
- Resetting a bracket keeps the matches players already booked, a result already entered on Challonge is never overwritten, a result Challonge drops gets retried, and re-matching an entrant moves their booked matches with them.
