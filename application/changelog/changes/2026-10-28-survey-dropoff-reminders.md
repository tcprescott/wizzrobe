### New
- Survey results open with a drop-off card: how many people opened, started and sent the survey, which parts they reached, where the rest stopped, and the median time to finish against the estimate.
- Survey invites and reminders have a **Not interested** button. Pressing it stops the reminders; the survey link still works.
- An open survey's Actions menu has **Change close date…**.

### Improved
- Survey DMs greet people by name and say how long their own survey takes. A reminder to someone who saved answers but never sent them says they're partway through.
- A survey can send two reminders, and the second is worded as a last call.
- The builder's "too long" warning now starts at 10 minutes instead of 5.
