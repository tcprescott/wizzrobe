### New

- Outside apps can now sign you in with Wizzrobe, where a community has Third-party Apps turned on. One card shows who made the app, where it sends you and what it asks for: reads start ticked, changes start unticked, and it only ever works in the community you pick.
- Apps you've connected sit with your API tokens on Profile, tagged App, and disconnect with one press. Profile also has a Developer apps section for registering an app of your own.
- Staff can see which apps members connected under Admin → Settings → Connected apps, and block one for the whole community.
