Wizzrobe runs schedules, brackets, crew and volunteers for speedrun and randomizer events. This page says what it keeps about you, who can see it, who else it goes to, and how to get it changed or removed. Each community on Wizzrobe is run by its own staff, who decide how they use what you share with their community.

## What we collect

When you sign in with Discord, Wizzrobe asks Discord for your basic profile only (the `identify` permission) and keeps your Discord ID, username and avatar. It doesn't keep your Discord password or your sign-in token, and it can't read your messages or servers.

Everything else is something you, or a community's staff, add:

- **Your profile:** a display name, pronouns and timezone.
- **Linked accounts** you choose to connect: Twitch, racetime.gg, Challonge, start.gg and Matcherino. We keep each account's ID and username, not its password or sign-in token, and you can unlink any of them from your profile.
- **Notification settings:** an email address and a Telegram chat, only if you turn those on, and a push subscription for each device you allow notifications on (with a short description of the browser, so you can tell your devices apart).
- **Event activity:** matches you play or crew, results and times, comments, reschedule requests, availability, tournament sign-ups, volunteer shifts, equipment you lend or borrow, and feedback you send.
- **Emergency contact and accessibility requests**, if you give them. These are only visible to the community's staff, and every time staff open your emergency contact you can see that they did.
- **Event check-in:** if a community sells badges through Matcherino, staff import its roster (name, Matcherino account and the Discord or Twitch account used to buy) to check you in. We drop the buyer's email, phone, address and payment details when we import it.

Some people get an account before they ever sign in: a community can bring players in from the SpeedGaming schedule, a start.gg bracket, or its Discord server's roles. Those accounts hold the name and Discord ID those services published.

## How the app uses cookies

Wizzrobe sets three cookies, all for running the app, none for advertising:

- `session` keeps you signed in, for 90 days after your last visit.
- `wiz_tz` remembers your device's timezone, so times show in your local time.
- `wiz_dark` remembers whether you picked dark mode.

The app also records which pages and buttons get used (never what you type into them), tied to your account if you're signed in. Staff read it to learn which parts of the app people use. We don't store your IP address in the database.

## Who can see it

- **Anyone**, signed in or not, can see schedules, brackets, results and the names of the players in them.
- **Members of your community** can see what the app shows on its schedule and crew pages, such as who's crewing a match.
- **Your community's staff and volunteers** can see what you've shared with that community, to run the event. Staff also see a log of changes made in the app.
- **Staff of other communities can't** see what you've shared with a community they don't run.

We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

## Services we share it with

Wizzrobe hands the minimum each service needs to do its job:

- **Discord**, for sign-in, direct messages, server roles and event listings.
- **SendGrid**, to send email, if you turn email on.
- **Telegram**, to send messages, if you connect it.
- **Your browser's push service** (Google, Apple or Mozilla), to deliver notifications to devices you allow.
- **Sentry**, which receives error reports and performance data to help us fix problems. A report can include your Discord ID and username.
- **racetime.gg, Twitch, Challonge, start.gg and Matcherino**, when you link those accounts or a community uses them for its tournaments.
- **Webhooks and AI assistants a community's staff connect**, which receive the community's event data. Webhooks include the username of whoever made a change.

## Where it's stored

Your information is stored on servers in the United States, and the services above process it in the United States too. If you use Wizzrobe from outside the US, your information is transferred to and stored there.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account and activity for as long as Wizzrobe runs your community's events, so past results and schedules stay accurate. You can remove your email, Telegram, push devices, linked accounts, emergency contact, accessibility request and availability yourself at any time from your profile. Emergency contacts and accessibility requests belong to one event: 30 days after staff archive that event, they're deleted.

The change log and usage records keep a copy of your username even after your account is gone, so the history of an event stays readable.

## Your choices

To see what we hold about you, correct it, or have your account deleted, use **Send Feedback** in the side menu when you're signed in. If you can't sign in, ask your community's staff on their Discord server. Leaving a community removes your emergency contact and closes any accessibility request you had open there.

## Children

Wizzrobe isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly keep information about them. If you think a child has signed up, tell us through Send Feedback and we'll remove the account.
